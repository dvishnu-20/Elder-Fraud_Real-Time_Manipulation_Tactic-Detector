# Phase 10 — Scientific Claim Audit

## Objective
Review all project reports to ensure scientific objectivity and remove any hyperbolic claims before finalizing the research version.

## Terminology Corrections Applied
- Replaced instances of *"solved real-world generalization"* with *"substantially improved performance on the evaluated realistic benchmark."*
- Replaced instances of *"production ready"* with *"ready for controlled research/pilot evaluation."*
- Replaced *"guaranteed safe"* with *"showed low false-positive rates under the evaluated protocol."*
- Replaced *"flawless integration"* with *"successful system integration with no observed state contamination."*
- Replaced *"strong temporal superiority"* with *"the frozen temporal model provides causal prefix-based risk estimation and modest temporal value."*

## Result
All claims in the final reporting layer have been audited and aligned with scientifically defensible wording based exclusively on the empirical measurements recorded in Phases 5-9.
